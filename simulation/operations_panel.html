Unattended training

First test the run, then click Stop & save on its training page. Select the saved run here and start unattended operation. It continues without a round limit, saves checkpoints and restarts after process failures.

Continue after logout or reboot

Linux may require desktop authentication for this setting. Keep the PC powered and disable automatic suspend in its power settings. The webpage does not change sleep settings. Closing the browser does not stop the service. Reopen this dashboard to monitor it.
